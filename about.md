I'm Elidio, a Cloud & Security student in Belgium with a particular interest in blue teaming.

I enjoy working through logs, understanding how an attack happened, and thinking about how to detect it. This blog is where I share what I learn through study, labs, and research.

## Why this blog?

Writing helps me make sense of what I'm learning. The guarded kernel is a place to work through an investigation, explain a detection idea, or take a closer look at a threat group's techniques.

I want the articles to be practical and clear: what happened, what the evidence shows, and what a defender can learn from it.

## What I'm interested in

- **Incident analysis:** following evidence and reconstructing an attack.
- **Detection engineering:** turning attacker behaviour into useful detection logic.
- **Threat research:** understanding APT techniques and their defensive implications.
